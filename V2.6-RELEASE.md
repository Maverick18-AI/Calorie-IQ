# CalorieIQ 2.6 — Barcode ingredients and live recipe nutrition

Build **2.6.0-dev.1**, development generation **7**. Started from the clean
published 2.5.0-dev.2 checkpoint `d37b619f70f5ab74513c0fde1608a88d7991610b`,
after fetching the remote. Work uses `development/v2`, then a history-preserving
fast-forward to `main` and the existing root GitHub Pages deployment. Publication
was explicitly authorized in the recipe-scanning assignment.

## Recipe workflow

- Recipes has Search ingredient, Scan Barcode and Custom ingredient actions.
  They reuse the existing food search, providers, camera/photo scanner and food
  review. No separate scanner, paid service, new database or package was added.
- Recipe entry highlights Recipes, labels the amount as quantity used in the
  recipe, hides the irrelevant diary meal control, and confirms directly into the
  recipe draft. It does not add a diary entry. Other ingredients, name, yield and
  the identity of an edited saved recipe remain intact.
- The review retains provider, brand, barcode, original nutrient basis, serving,
  warnings and provenance. Saved exact/padded barcode corrections retain priority.
  Save to my foods also works when confirming an ingredient or label correction.
- Unknown/unavailable lookups leave the recipe untouched and offer manual entry.
  Back to recipe retains unfinished ingredient review; Continue ingredient review
  resumes it. Cancel ingredient discards only that pending ingredient. A nonempty
  pending review must be completed/canceled before saving the recipe.
- Ingredient amounts update immediately in the builder. Compatible unit changes
  preserve the physical amount; unsupported mass/volume changes are rejected.
  Whole-package quantities use existing verified package conversions. Invalid
  amount fields remain visible through navigation/reload and block recipe saving;
  they do not silently become zero or the previous valid amount.
- Edit ingredient opens full review; Replace with barcode replaces one selected
  slot. Its original slot/ID must still match. Other ingredients and historical
  Food Log snapshots are not rewritten. Separate deliberate additions of the same
  product are allowed; accepted review operations are consumed once.

## Live nutrition and data

Entire recipe and Per serving columns update on add, removal, amount/unit changes
and recipe yield. Primary macros are visible; fiber, sugars, sodium and other
tracked nutrients are available under a compact disclosure. Table headers have
row/column scopes. Missing nutrition remains unknown (—), not zero. A nutrient is
shown only when all ingredients supply it, matching saved-recipe calculation.
Invalid yields cannot divide by zero or display a misleading per-serving result.
Displayed values use the existing one-decimal formatting; calculations and saved
snapshots retain their unrounded precision.

The user's illustrative cheese label, 110 kcal per 28 g serving, is covered by
clearly synthetic label tests: 56 g = 220 kcal, half a serving = 55 kcal, and an
explicit eight-serving package = 880 kcal. These fixtures are not published as
manufacturer nutrition facts. Real USDA/OFF fixtures and previous accuracy tests
remain unchanged. Source data and nutrition are never averaged across products.

`recipes.js` contains small pure draft-nutrition and ingredient-replacement
helpers; persisted recipes still use the existing core schema and snapshots.
Session draft version 1 adds optional ingredient context and raw amount fields;
older session drafts and version-2 backups remain supported. Invalid/unreadable
session drafts retain their downloadable recovery path. No new diary-storage key,
backup format, migration or historical-nutrition rewrite was introduced.

## Physical iPhone acceptance

1. Open the existing HTTPS website, confirm 2.6.0-dev.1, and update through Goals →
   App & privacy if needed. Close old tabs; this is a new development generation.
2. In Recipes, enter a name/yield, add several ingredients, then tap Scan Barcode.
   Allow camera access and scan a real packaged food. Review its physical label,
   quantity basis and grams/ml before confirming. Try a fractional quantity.
3. Stop/cancel scanning, deny permission, background/return and switch to Search.
   Confirm existing ingredients/name/yield remain and the camera indicator stops.
4. Adjust an ingredient and yield; compare entire/per-serving totals. Save, reopen,
   replace one ingredient by scanning and log the finished recipe in Food Log.
5. Test a corrected product, an unknown barcode, package quantities, an offline
   saved barcode, backup export/restore and same-build offline reload.

Do not clear Safari website data merely to install the update. Nutrition labels,
camera reliability, iOS Files behaviour and actual offline availability need the
physical checks above; desktop simulation cannot certify them.
