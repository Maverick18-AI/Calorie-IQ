## Weight and profile

- Obvious Log weight action; dated entry creation, editing, deletion and Undo.
  Moving an edited entry to another date removes its original record; replacing
  another date's record requires confirmation. Edit identity survives draft recovery.
- Pounds are the default. Entered weights and weekly rates convert when changing
  units. Preferred weight units persist; existing stored kilogram values are not
  relabelled. Storage remains metric internally for calculation consistency.
- Starting and goal weight controls are reachable from Weight's summary. A saved
  starting point is separate from recorded weigh-ins; current weight comes from
  the latest weigh-in through today. Future dates are excluded from current/trend.
- 7/30/90-day and all-time charts, optional trailing seven-day average, axis labels,
  and point selection by touch, pointer or keyboard. Exact history remains listed.
- A local personal profile stores optional adult age, height, equation sex option,
  activity and preferred units. Height uses feet and inches. Changing the profile's
  current weight creates/replaces today's weigh-in with confirmation when needed.
- No new family accounts, authentication or synchronization. Existing archived
  profiles remain in backups; personal fields belong to their respective profile.

## Calorie target method and safeguards

Mifflin–St Jeor estimates resting energy; existing activity multipliers estimate
maintenance. Lose/maintain/gain and the preferred rate produce a suggested target.
The UI shows resting energy, maintenance, daily deficit/surplus and suggested target.
Saving a profile or preference does not change calorie goals. Use suggested target
fills the goal form; Save goals applies it to Food Log/Nutrition, with existing
date-specific overrides retained. Manual target entry needs no equation sex option.

The rate adjustment uses 7,700 kcal/kg as a planning approximation, not a forecast.
The blank-rate default is 0.5 lb/week. Requested loss is limited to 2 lb/week and
gain to 1 lb/week. Loss deficits are capped at 25% of maintenance; gain surplus is
capped at 500 kcal/day. App guardrails use 1,200 kcal/day minimum, or 1,500 with
the saved male coefficient, and 10,000 maximum for newly entered targets. These
are conservative product bounds, not universal clinical safety thresholds.
Estimates below the bounds, unsupported ages/measurements, conflicting goal
directions and underweight loss goals are rejected. A limited rate is explained.
Existing imported historical goals are not silently rewritten.

Equation calculations are for adults 18–100 and are labelled estimates, unsuitable
for pregnancy, breastfeeding or medically supervised diets. Activity factors and
fixed rate arithmetic have individual uncertainty. Age is a saved value, not an
automatically aging date of birth. Saving a new weigh-in does not silently retune
the calorie goal. Recalculate and explicitly apply when desired.

Sources: [original equation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/),
[NHLBI weight-planning guidance](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/files/docs/guidelines/ob_gdlns.pdf),
[CDC gradual weight loss](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/).

## Backups, branding and service worker

Settings → Data & Backups is collapsed by default with Export backup and Restore
backup. Less-common recovery/editing tools are nested further. Validation, restore
confirmation, recovery snapshots, conflict protection and draft downloads remain.
The version-2 backup/storage keys remain; optional `personal` and starting-point
fields are validated. Old backups without them still work. No nutrition database,
barcode source, serving conversion or diary snapshot rewrite was introduced.

The app uses pumpkin #FF7518, black, white and warm neutrals in both themes. Blue
branding was removed from CSS, logo, icons and manifest. Text contrast is tested;
links use a darker orange on light backgrounds. The icon is an original SVG plate/
fork mark, exported with existing Edge at 180/192/512 px using
`scripts/export-icons.cjs` and the existing optional Playwright runtime. No package,
font, image-generation service or paid dependency was added.

New icon filenames are used by the favicon/header, Apple touch link and manifest.
Legacy icon filenames also contain the new artwork. New assets are included in the
versioned shell; the large USDA catalogue remains excluded from precaching. There
were no existing custom iOS launch images; manifest background and touch/PWA
branding are updated without inventing device-specific splash assets.

Development mode is retained: generation 5 resets CalorieIQ test data once on
installation. Same-build reopen/reload preserves subsequent records. Tests run in
disposable contexts and do not clear the owner's browser. Export anything wanted
before installing a new development generation. No raw data or unrelated files
were deleted by development tools.
