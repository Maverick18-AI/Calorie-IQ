Goals & settings

Your profile stays on this device. Saving a profile or weight preference does not change your calorie goal.

Personal profile

A changed current weight is logged for today. Historical weigh-ins are retained.

Weight goal & starting point

Choose up to 2 lb/week for loss or 1 lb/week for gain. A blank rate uses 0.5 lb/week. The calculator may suggest a slower rate.

Calorie target estimate

For adults 18–100. Mifflin–St Jeor estimates resting energy; activity and goal adjustments are approximate. Not for pregnancy, breastfeeding or medically supervised diets.

This fills the goal form only. Review and save to apply. No automatic goal changes. Equation source · Weight-planning guidance

Daily goals

Optional nutrient targets

Data & Backups

Logs and profile information stay on this device. Keep an exported copy in Files. Backups are unencrypted and include all stored records.

Restore validates the file before replacing data and asks for confirmation. A recovery copy of current data is kept first.

Recovery & editing tools
App & privacy

Development mode: each new development build resets CalorieIQ test data once. Reopening the same build preserves new entries.

No accounts, ads, or subscriptions. Searches and barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts. USDA uses reference files from this website. Camera images are decoded on your device.

Install on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Saved foods and downloaded USDA details work offline after caching. An uncached product lookup still needs the internet.
