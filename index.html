Backups & recovery

Your data lives on this device. Browser cleanup or device loss can erase it. Backups include every family profile and are unencrypted.

App & privacy

No accounts, ads, or subscriptions. Search terms and barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts. Camera images are decoded on your device.

Install on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Saved foods, recipes, and barcode scanning work offline after the app is cached.
