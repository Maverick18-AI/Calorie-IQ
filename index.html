Backups & recovery

Your data lives on this device. Browser cleanup or device loss can erase it. Backups are unencrypted and retain all stored records, including any profiles from earlier versions.

App & privacy

Development mode: each new development build resets CalorieIQ test data once. Reopening the same build preserves new entries.

No accounts, ads, or subscriptions. Search terms and barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts. USDA search uses downloadable reference files from this website; no API key is required. Camera images are decoded on your device.

Install on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Saved foods, recipes, and barcode scanning work offline after the app is cached.
